# Widgingarri Shelter 1: 2022 excavation finds 3D viewer

## Overview

This repository contains an interactive 3D visualization of archaeological finds recovered during the 2022 excavations at **Widgingarri Shelter 1** in the Kimberley region of northwest Australia.

**Widgingarri Shelter 1** is a culturally significant site of the Arraluli people. The 2022 excavation was a collaboration between the Arraluli and the Australian Research Council Centre of Excellence for Australian Biodiversity and Heritage.

[!IMPORTANT]
The visualization presents archaeological data from a culturally significant site of the Arraluli people, and is shared in support of the publications listed below.

The visualization accompanies and supplements the following publications:

- Hayes et al. (submitted). Tool use, maintenance and pigment processing at Widgingarri Shelter 1: Insights into Aboriginal technological practices from ≥55,000 years ago.
- Lin et al. (2026). Recent archaeological investigations at Widgingarri Shelter 1, northwest Australia. *Australian Archaeology*.

The interactive plot allows users to explore the three-dimensional spatial distribution of artefacts recovered from the excavation. Individual finds are plotted according to their recorded excavation coordinates and can be examined interactively within a web browser.

## Viewing the Visualisation

Download the repository and open `Widgingarri_3D_Plot.html` in a modern web browser.

Recommended browsers:
- Google Chrome
- Microsoft Edge
- Mozilla Firefox
- Safari

 
No additional software installation is required.


## Navigation

The visualization can be explored using standard mouse or trackpad controls:
- Click and drag to rotate the view.
- Scroll to zoom in and out.
- Right-click and drag to pan.
- Hover over a point to display specimen information.
- Single left-click on legend item to exclude from display.
- Double left-click on legend item to only display the selected item.


## Data Description

The plot displays the recorded three-dimensional positions of piece-plotted archaeological finds recovered during the 2022 excavation of Widgingarri Shelter 1.

Finds are colour-coded by artefact category and include both piece-provenienced specimens and materials recovered through sieving. Categories represented in the dataset include:

- Lithics
- Grinding stone fragments
- Edge-ground hatchet (EGH) flakes
- A single possible EGH fragment
- Haematite (pigment)

Each plotted point represents an individual archaeological find located according to its recorded excavation coordinates.

The visualization includes a semi-transparent three-dimensional surface representing the excavated deposit, allowing archaeological finds to be viewed within their broader spatial context.

Hovering over an individual point displays:
- X coordinate
- Y coordinate
- Z coordinate
- Unit-ID

The Unit-ID corresponds to the unique identifier assigned to each archaeological find within the excavation database.

## Notes on Provenience

The visualization includes materials recovered through both piece-proveniencing and sieving. Consequently, spatial precision varies among artefact classes and recovery methods. Users should consider these differences when interpreting fine-scale spatial patterning within the deposit. 

See Lin et al. (2026) for more information on the excavation and recording strategy.
